Where it breaks down, and how to fix it

Seven places where the process above asks more of a student than the task requires — paired with the smallest change that would remove the friction.

Friction

Planning a schedule and booking it are two disconnected tools. A student can build a perfect schedule in the Planner, then has to manually re-find and re-select every section in TSS to actually book it.

Fix

Let "Book this schedule" hand the Planner's selections to TSS as a pre-filled booking queue, so the plan becomes the cart instead of a list to retype.

Friction

The one fact every student needs first — when their booking window opens — sits three clicks into TSS (search → My Appointment Times → choose term), and it changes every quarter without any notice.

Fix

Show the next booking window as a standing banner on the TSS homepage the moment a student logs in, and send a reminder 24 hours before it opens.

Friction

WebReg and MyTritonLink stay live alongside TSS through Summer 2026, so a student searching for help can land on instructions for a system they're not using, with different terminology ("enroll" vs. "book").

Fix

Redirect legacy help pages to their TSS equivalent for the duration of the transition, and standardize on one verb — "book" — everywhere, including old content.

Friction

A blocked booking (hold, unmet prerequisite, or unit cap) dead-ends into a separate TEA request with no visible status once it's submitted — the student just has to check back later.

Fix

Start the TEA request right from the failed "Book/Save" attempt, pre-filled with the course and reason, and show pending / approved / denied on that course's card.

Friction

Unit caps shift by pass, by student level, and by week — 11.5 → 19.5 → 22 units for undergrads, 20 → 30 for grad students — and the student has to remember which limit applies today.

Fix

Replace the static limit text with a live "X of Y units used this pass" counter on the booking screen, calculated automatically from the student's level and the date.

Friction

Opening a second TSS tab can lock the account or freeze registration — a backend limitation turned into a rule the student has to remember at the most time-pressured moment of the quarter.

Fix

Detect a second active session server-side and show "You're already logged in elsewhere" instead of letting the interface fail silently.

Friction

Holds are checked as a separate, easy-to-skip errand days before booking opens, rather than being caught at the moment they'd actually stop something.

Fix

Show any active hold directly on the appointment-time card and at the top of the booking screen, with a one-click link to resolve it.
